02 / The Idea

Photographs
as objects.

A photograph can be more than an image. It can hold a place, a person, a feeling and a piece of time.

FOLIA treats every collection as a small visual object: something that can be explored, revisited and remembered. The pages are designed like chapters in an archive rather than a service catalogue.

Browse the collection, open a story, and move through the photographs at your own pace.
